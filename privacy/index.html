Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how YCC Personal Command Center ("PCC", "we", "our" or "the application") handles information when an authorized user connects a TikTok account or uses this legal website. PCC is currently a private, local-first personal operations and analytics system rather than a public consumer service.

1. Information PCC may access from TikTok

When you deliberately authorize a TikTok account, PCC may request only the permissions configured for the integration. The initial integration is designed to use read-only access such as:

  • basic TikTok account identity and profile information authorized through TikTok Login Kit;
  • recent public video information made available through TikTok's API;
  • authorization metadata needed to maintain the connection, including access and refresh tokens.

PCC does not request TikTok direct messages, passwords, private drafts, payment information, or permission to publish videos as part of this initial analytics connection.

2. How TikTok information is used

Authorized TikTok information is used to provide private analytics and operating intelligence inside PCC, including identifying the connected account, displaying recent content, supporting campaign analysis, and combining available social-performance evidence with the user's own PCC records.

PCC does not sell TikTok data, rent it, or use it to build advertising profiles for third parties.

3. Where information is stored

PCC is designed as a local-first application. TikTok authorization credentials and retrieved PCC records are stored on the user's own computer in PCC's private application storage. Credential files are intended to use restrictive local file permissions and are not displayed back in the normal dashboard after they are saved.

TikTok's own systems necessarily receive authentication and API requests when the user connects or refreshes TikTok data. PCC may also connect to other third-party services when the user separately enables them. TikTok data is not intentionally sent to another external service merely because the TikTok connection exists.

4. Data retention

Locally stored TikTok information may remain in PCC until the user disconnects the TikTok integration, deletes the associated local records, or removes the PCC installation and its data. Some locally stored historical analytics may remain after authorization expires unless the user deletes them.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information may be disclosed only when the user deliberately enables a workflow that requires another service, when necessary to communicate with TikTok's API, when required by law, or when needed to protect the security and integrity of the system.

6. Security

PCC uses a local-first architecture and is designed to run its dashboard on the local computer rather than exposing the application directly to the public internet. Reasonable safeguards are used for locally stored credentials and application data. However, no computer system, storage method, or network connection can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your choices and control

You control whether a TikTok account is connected. You may stop PCC from accessing TikTok by disconnecting the integration in PCC and/or revoking the application's access through your TikTok account settings. You may also delete locally stored PCC data from the computer on which PCC is installed.

8. Third-party services

TikTok is operated by a third party and is governed by TikTok's own terms, privacy policy, developer rules, API availability, and account controls. Other optional PCC integrations are likewise governed by their respective providers. This Privacy Policy does not replace those third-party policies.

9. Children's privacy

PCC is not designed as a service for children and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children below the minimum age required by applicable law or the connected platform.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when PCC's functionality or integrations materially change. The effective date at the top of the page will be updated when revisions are published.

11. Contact

Privacy questions may be directed to the operator of YCC Personal Command Center using the developer contact information registered for the YCC PCC application in TikTok for Developers.

PCC is currently a private local-first system. If PCC later becomes a public or commercial service, this policy should be reviewed and expanded before public onboarding begins.
